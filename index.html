The Jayagoo spreadsheet guide

A simple method.
A clearer shortlist.

Use this four-step routine for Jayagoo spreadsheet finds. Keep product facts separate from your own preferences so you can revisit a decision without repeating the same research.

01

Choose a category

Begin with shoes, clothing, headwear, or accessories. Give each category its own section so unrelated products are easier to compare.

02

Save the source

Record the exact listing URL, selected variation, seller name, and date checked. Similar photographs can appear across different listings.

03

Compare the essentials

Check measurements, materials, available options, return terms, and complete delivered cost. Mark missing information as unknown.

04

Review before buying

Reopen the listing to confirm current details. A saved note is a research aid; prices, sizes, and availability may have changed.

The product comparison sheet

What deserves a column?

A useful Jayagoo spreadsheet product list captures enough detail to explain why an item made your shortlist. These fields work for both a small personal collection and a larger fashion research sheet.

FieldRecord thisWhy it helps
Product sourceListing URL, seller, selected variant, date checkedKeeps each note connected to the exact item you reviewed.
Sizing & fitSize chart, garment measurements, shoe length, intended fitHelps compare products whose labeled sizes differ.
Material & finishStated composition, lining, hardware, care instructionsSeparates documented specifications from visual impressions.
Images & evidenceDetail photos, seller disclosures, documentation where relevantHighlights unanswered questions before you commit.
Total costItem price, currency, shipping, applicable fees and taxesSupports a more realistic budget comparison.
Purchase conditionsAvailability, dispatch estimate, returns, cancellation termsMakes practical limitations visible alongside style preferences.

A useful rule: if a listing does not establish a detail, write “not confirmed.” A photograph alone cannot verify authenticity, composition, or quality.

Questions, answered

A little clarity
before you browse.

Practical answers for using a Jayagoo spreadsheet guide and keeping product research organized.

What is a Jayagoo spreadsheet?

In this guide, Jayagoo spreadsheet describes a product-link research workflow: organizing fashion finds by category, recording listing details, and comparing options before purchase. This page is an independent guide, not an official Jayagoo service or a live spreadsheet.

How do I use a Jayagoo spreadsheet guide?

Choose a category, open a product listing, record its URL and relevant details, then compare measurements, materials, price, shipping, and return terms before making a decision.

Does a product photo prove authenticity?

No. A product photo or brand name alone does not establish authenticity, seller authorization, material quality, or current availability. Check reliable documentation and the individual seller’s terms.

Are listed prices the complete delivered cost?

Not necessarily. Depending on the transaction, shipping, service fees, taxes, duties, and currency conversion may add to the item price. Confirm the complete payable amount before checkout.
